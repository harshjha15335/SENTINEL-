# SENTINEL narration

Delivery: composed documentary tone; conversational, precise. Allow the images to breathe. Names: SENTINEL (SEN-ti-nel), XGBoost (ex gee boost), SHAP (shap), Gemini (JEM-in-eye), Supabase (SOO-puh-base).

## 000–022 seconds — intro

A payment takes seconds. Understanding it can take much longer. A sudden burst of activity. An unusual amount. Records scattered across different systems. SENTINEL brings those signals together, so an analyst can see what happened—and decide what comes next.

## 022–028 seconds — landing

Follow the money, from the payment to the evidence behind it.

## 028–036 seconds — dashboard

The Command Center brings flagged payments into focus, with the context an analyst needs to begin.

## 036–044 seconds — transactions

Open a transaction. See its payment history, risk assessment, and the case it belongs to.

## 044–057 seconds — risk

Here, the X G boost model assigns a forty-six point five percent risk score. The case is synthetic. The model runs in shadow mode, supporting review rather than moving money.

## 057–064 seconds — shap

Shap contributions explain which signals pushed this result up or down.

## 064–084 seconds — gemini

In this separate hosted run, Gemini completes a real investigation. Seven tools read the available records and return grounded findings. Citations make the reasoning reviewable. The recommendation: request more evidence. A person still decides what happens next.

## 084–092 seconds — tools

Open the tool trace. See which records the agent read, and follow a finding back to its source.

## 092–104 seconds — evidence

Bring documents into the case, keep their provenance, and generate a reviewable P D F packet. The evidence stays connected to the investigation.

## 104–112 seconds — approval

Decision requests and independent approvals keep the next step accountable. No financial action is executed here.

## 112–120 seconds — dispute

Dispute Copilot prepares a grounded response. Review the evidence. External submission stays disabled.

## 120–132 seconds — policy

In Policy Lab, adjust a threshold and inspect the trade-off. Review the R O C and precision recall analysis against held-out synthetic data, before considering a policy change.

## 132–141 seconds — monitor

Risk Monitoring compares activity and score distributions over time. A shift is a reason to investigate, not a verdict.

## 141–150 seconds — network

Relationship Explorer connects payers and payments. Select a relationship, inspect the recorded history, and bring the context back to the case.

## 150–162 seconds — integration

Earlier audits verified a genuine PayPal sandbox capture and processed Supa base events. These recorded results are separate from this synthetic walkthrough.

## 162–172 seconds — closing

SENTINEL. Detection, explanation, and investigation. One connected view. Human judgment at the center.
